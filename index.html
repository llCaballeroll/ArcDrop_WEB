Drop fast or hold

Send now, or collect for later.

Let go as soon as you reach the icon to send. Keep the file over it for a moment and it goes to your tray. Two small drop zones appear right under the icon, so you never push against the top edge where Mission Control opens.

  • Choose the hold time in Settings
  • Use the icon, the zones or both

Your tray

Gather files from anywhere.

A logo from Downloads, a quote from Documents, a screenshot. Add them in several moves, check thumbnails and sizes, preview with Quick Look and send everything together. Your originals never move.

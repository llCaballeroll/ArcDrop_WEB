Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short

  • ArcDrop does not collect any data. We have no servers, accounts, analytics, ads or trackers, and the app has no network code.
  • Your files stay on your Mac. ArcDrop only hands them to the system AirDrop or share sheet when you choose to send them.
  • Your tray and send history are saved only on your Mac, and you can delete them at any time.

Files you share

When you drop, paste or choose files, ArcDrop keeps a reference to them so it can show thumbnails and send them. Copies it creates (captures, prepared images, annotated images, ZIP files) are stored inside the app's own folder on your Mac and are removed when you take them out of the tray or delete them from the history.

Screen Recording permission

macOS asks you to allow Screen Recording the first time you use region capture. ArcDrop only looks at the screen while you select a region, and the image never leaves your Mac unless you send it.

Data we collect

None. We never receive your files, captures, history, settings or any personal information.

Purchases

ArcDrop Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment and the Apple ID it uses; we never receive your name, email or payment details.

AirDrop and sharing

Sending uses Apple's AirDrop and share sheet. What happens after you pick a device or an app is handled by Apple and that app, under their own privacy policies.

Children

ArcDrop is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. You can clear the tray and the history in ArcDrop, and uninstalling the app removes all its data from your Mac.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

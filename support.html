Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to soporte.arcdrop@outlook.com. Tell us your Mac model, macOS version and what you were doing. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

I cannot see the ArcDrop icon.

The menu bar may be full or hidden behind the notch. Hold Command and drag other icons out of the menu bar, or open ArcDrop again from Applications to see its settings.

Dropping a file opens Mission Control.

That happens when a file is held against the top edge of the screen. Drop it on the ArcDrop icon right away, or use the small Send and Tray zones that appear under the icon. You can turn them on in Settings › Drag & Send.

How do I add files to the tray instead of sending them?

Keep the file over the icon for about a second until it turns into a tray, or drop it on the Tray zone. You can also right-click files in Finder and choose Add to ArcDrop.

Region capture does not work.

Allow ArcDrop in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, then quit and reopen ArcDrop.

Add to ArcDrop does not appear in Finder.

Right-click the files and look under Quick Actions or Services. If it is missing, turn it on in System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts › Services.

I bought ArcDrop Pro. How do I get it on another Mac?

Open ArcDrop Settings › General and click "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID.
